# CEO review

## Verdict: scope reduction
The original “film a house and AI tells you what is valuable” pitch creates a trust and accuracy trap. ClearKind should sell risk reduction and family coordination. The winning first product is a do-not-discard exception packet, not an appraisal engine or junk-hauling affiliate funnel.

## Challenges and decisions
- **Premise:** wealth transfer is a macro tailwind, not demand proof. Decision: require paid concierge evidence.
- **Wedge:** sorting everything is too broad. Decision: find the small set that must be held or reviewed.
- **ICP/GTM:** one-off consumer CAC is dangerous. Decision: start with estate attorneys, senior move managers, and listing agents after manual proof.
- **Economics:** affiliate revenue can corrupt trust. Decision: charge the family directly; disclose any later referral economics.
- **Moat:** vision models commoditize. Decision: build consent history, outcome data, and provider reliability.
- **Architecture:** do not duplicate opaque model logic. Decision: deterministic route engine, explicit fixtures, API error names, and eventual versioned decision events.
- **Observability:** track reversals and discarded-value incidents, not just scans or routes.
- **Failure modes:** nil inputs, empty rooms, family disagreement, stale decisions, slow uploads, specialist unavailability, and double submission all need visible states before production.

## 12-month ideal
Current scattered texts → paid exception packet → trusted case record used by families and estate professionals. The demo advances the interaction and rule transparency without pretending the hardest recognition or trust problems are solved.
